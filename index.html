Before you read the numbers

This is a proposal for a new, separate facility. The Project Clinic is a student-operated project laboratory. It is not an extension of LHC 103 / 203 / 303, and it does not draw on their stock — those labs need what they hold for timetabled teaching.

So nothing is netted off. Every line is costed at its full requirement. What the department already owns is shown alongside purely as context, so the committee can see where an item exists elsewhere and decide, separately, whether to transfer it rather than buy it. That decision belongs to the department; no total on this page assumes it.

Figures are read live from the department Proposal_Data. Prices are indicative for budgeting and require three quotations before any purchase order.
