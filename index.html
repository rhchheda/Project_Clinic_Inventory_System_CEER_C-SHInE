Position at a glance

How to read this. Every proposed line is shown against what the department already holds, for reference only. HELD = it already holds this quantity or more somewhere. SOME = it holds a lesser quantity. NONE = it holds none.

The Project Clinic is a separate facility, so nothing is netted off. Every line is costed at its full requirement, because stock in LHC 103 / 203 / 303, the Store Room, the Project Lab or the Tinkering Lab belongs to those facilities and they still need it. The existing-stock columns are context for the committee, not a deduction. Where a transfer genuinely makes sense it is listed under Redistribution before purchase as an explicit option. Prices are indicative and require three quotations before purchase.
